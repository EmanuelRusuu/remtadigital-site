What I built

  • A release-date product filter that renders near-instantly using CSS instead of reloading the grid.
  • The auction page, and full player names in shirt personalisation.
  • The club's Abacus job portal embedded in a Shopify page template.
  • FCSG TV video pages, and a kids club membership form pre-filled from promotion emails.
  • Sold-out products that keep an open product page, and unlisted products in featured collections.
