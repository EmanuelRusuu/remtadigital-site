What I built

  • Performance: image attributes and sizing to remove layout shift, minified files and fewer analytics requests.
  • Collection filtering: price filters, sorting, pagination, applied-filter chips and a separate mobile filter, with skeleton loading.
  • Match listings sorted by date, login and logout links in the footer, women's team sponsors and camp signup deadlines.
