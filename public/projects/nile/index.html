Nile

A Swiss fashion retailer running six Shopify stores from one shared theme: CHF, EUR and GBP, each with a test and a production store.

I worked on this as a developer at aiconomy AG, which ran the project for its client. REMTA Digital was not involved; the details below describe my own part.

What I built

  • Loyalty program (Nilecard): a theme-driven points system with per-store redemption values, maximum points and fallback logic, plus points balance and history in the customer account.
  • Multi-store and localisation: the GBP store pair added to the build pipeline, cross-store country switching that remembers the shopper's choice, and per-store Klaviyo endpoints.
  • Customer account: order history and order detail with payment method and transaction breakdown.
  • Search: an InstantSearch implementation for the search page and predictive search, with progressive rendering and skeleton loading.
  • SEO: JSON-LD structured data for products, collections and breadcrumbs, including return policy, shipping and delivery-time data, with locale-aware schema and canonical URLs.
