What was built

  • Product page: tabbed product information (ingredients, nutritional values, declaration), each tab its own snippet, with empty tabs hidden automatically so a product without nutrition data never shows a blank tab.
  • Breadcrumbs, title, price and a transport and delivery-period block.
  • A grid card selector for product options, a product video modal and a related products block.
  • Product badges, including a "New" badge driven by product data.
  • Collection page: banner and breadcrumbs, a sidebar filter layout for desktop and a separate filter experience for mobile, and product cards rebuilt to match the brand's existing design.
  • Desktop mega menu, mobile navigation drawer and a reveal-on-scroll animation.
