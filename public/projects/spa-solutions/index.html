Spa Solutions

Custom discount and cart logic for a Swiss swim spa and cold plunge retailer, where the price depends on whether the customer installs the product themselves or books the installation team.

What was built

  • Discount buttons on the product page that map to dynamic Shopify discounts, so each installation choice shows its own price.
  • Installation (Montage) products added alongside the main product and kept in sync with it: same quantity, removed together, and never reachable on their own.
  • A cart drawer and cart page that recalculate prices and discounts live, with loading and disabled states so quantities cannot drift out of sync.
  • Only one discount active at a time, with the biggest one carried into checkout.
