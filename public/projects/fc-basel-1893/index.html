What was built

  • Club content on Shopify: a newest-videos page with category groups and skeleton loading, a faster league table, a "current / next match" button, player squads for the men's and women's teams, and the news pages with category filtering and a breaking news ticker.
  • Header and search: a sticky gradient header with mega menu and mobile drawer, and predictive search across products, players and news in one modal.
  • Jersey configurator: kit number fonts, a top or bottom name position, sponsor badges in the sponsor's brand font with brightness detection for contrast, per-digit pricing for custom numbers and a searchable player number dropdown.
  • Checkout bundling: a Cart Transform admin page and safety gate in the club's companion Shopify app.
  • Customer-specific discounted prices across product pages, collections and cart, stock rules so out-of-stock products cannot be bought, and purchase-disabled products with a notice.
