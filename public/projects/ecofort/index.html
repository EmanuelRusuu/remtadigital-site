Ecofort

Storefront for a Swiss supplier of heating and dehumidification products, plus theme repositories for the sister stores Infrarotheizung and Luftentfeuchter.

I worked on this as a developer at aiconomy AG, which ran the project for its client. REMTA Digital was not involved; the details below describe my own part.

What I built

  • Performance: critical CSS extracted, theme scripts deferred, LCP image and fonts preloaded, and the product image slider rebuilt to remove layout shift on mobile.
  • An accessibility pass covering colour contrast, link names and iframe titles.
  • Collection loading with skeleton states, and an advice and news loader with per-article SEO, a 404 for empty pages and inactive articles filtered out.
  • Trusted Shops ratings, a delivery status indicator on the product page and legacy product IDs carried through cart into checkout for tracking.
  • Credit check reliability: Intrum outages were being treated as declines. The backend now reports whether Intrum actually returned a decision, calls have timeouts so checkout never hangs, and checkout shows a neutral "temporarily unavailable" message.
