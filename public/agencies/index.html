What I take on

Theme work

New themes on Dawn or Horizon, and sections and fixes on existing themes, built so your client’s team can still edit them.

Custom functionality

Configurators, bundles, gift cards, wishlists and discount logic that Shopify doesn’t do out of the box.

Functions and checkout

Shopify Functions in TypeScript, checkout UI extensions and admin apps on the Admin GraphQL API.

Integrations

ERP, PIM, search and newsletter tools connected to Shopify, so the data stays consistent on both sides.

Migrations

Customers, orders and content moved in from other platforms, starting with a dry run.

Speed and clean-up

Heavy themes made faster and easier to maintain, without taking the store offline.

How I work with your team

  1. Your workflow. I work in your Git repository and on development themes, through your pull requests and code reviews.
  2. Your client stays yours. I can work under your agency’s name. You stay the client’s contact, and I join calls when you want me there.
  3. A clear estimate. A written estimate per task or per block of hours before I start, and I bill the hours I actually work.
  4. Nothing goes live without approval. The client’s live store stays untouched until you approve the changes.
