What I can build for you

Custom themes

A new theme on Dawn or Horizon, or careful work on the theme you already have, so it stays fast and your team can still edit it.

Custom functionality

Product configurators, bundles, loyalty points, gift cards, wishlists and custom discounts: the things Shopify doesn't do out of the box.

Integrations

Shopify connected to your ERP, PIM, search service or newsletter tool, built so the data stays consistent on both sides.

Shopify apps

Admin apps, checkout extensions and Shopify Functions, built on the Admin GraphQL API.

Speed and clean-up

Heavy themes made faster and easier to maintain, without taking the store offline.

About me

I got into web development through a front-end course in 2022 and 2023, and moved to Shopify in 2023. At WebGurus I built and rebuilt custom themes for US stores, and gradually took on the client side of projects too. At aiconomy AG I work on large, multilingual Swiss stores connected to the company's ERP, from sports clubs to fashion and food brands.

Along the way the job grew beyond writing code. I review colleagues' work, talk solutions through with project managers and present progress to clients, and I enjoy that part as much as the development itself. REMTA Digital is how I now take on projects of my own.

How I work

  • I agree on scope and trade-offs in plain language before I write code.
  • I build on a staging store and ship in small, reviewable steps.
  • Several languages and markets are the default for me, not an afterthought.
  • I leave code your own team can read and keep working on.

Questions people ask me

Is REMTA Digital an agency?

No. It's my one-person company, so you work with me directly. For bigger jobs I'm happy to work alongside your in-house team or your agency.

Can you work on our existing theme?

Yes. I can extend or clean up the theme you have, or build a new one if that's the better path.

Do you work with Shopify Plus and several markets?

Yes. Most of the stores I've worked on sell in several languages and currencies, including setups where several stores share one theme.

Can you connect Shopify to our ERP?

Usually, yes. Tell me which system you use and what needs to sync, and I'll explain how I'd approach it.

How do we get started?

Send me a short message about your store and what you'd like to build. I'll reply with a few questions and a suggestion for next steps.
