Services

What we build

</>

Custom themes

Storefronts built around your brand on Dawn, Horizon or your existing theme. Fast, accessible and easy for your team to maintain.

⚙

Custom functionality

Product configurators, wishlists, advanced filtering, loyalty points, discount logic and checkout bundling with Shopify Functions: rules that Shopify does not do out of the box.

⇄

ERP & system integrations

Reliable sync between your store and ERP, PIM, search and other systems, designed so data stays consistent.

▦

Custom Shopify apps

Admin apps for content sync across stores, data tooling and internal workflows, built with the Admin GraphQL API.

↺

Migrations

Moving customers, orders and content from other platforms, with dry runs, safeguards and a clear record of every row.

⚡

Performance & refactoring

Rebuilding or cleaning up heavy themes to improve speed and maintainability without disrupting a live store. Critical CSS, deferred scripts and no layout shift.

Process

How we work

  1. Discover

    We learn how your store and business run, what needs to connect, and what success looks like.

  2. Plan

    We agree on scope, approach and trade-offs in plain language before writing code.

  3. Build

    Iterative delivery with regular progress updates, code review and testing on a staging store.

  4. Launch & support

    A careful release, then follow-up so your team can run what we built with confidence.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you work on our existing theme?

Yes. We can extend or refactor an existing theme, or build a new one if that is the better path.

Do you work with Shopify Plus and multi-market stores?

Yes. Multilingual and multi-market setups are a core part of our experience.

Can you connect Shopify to our ERP?

Usually, yes. Tell us which system you use and what needs to sync, and we will tell you how we would approach it.

How do we get started?

Send a short message describing your project. We will reply with a few questions and suggest next steps.
